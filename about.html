Miyamoto Architecture is a design, interiors and architecture studio based in New York City.

Yuri Miyamoto is an architectural designer, researcher, and educator. She is licensed to practice architecture in the state of New York and has over ten years of experience working in a variety of multidisciplinary design offices. Her work spans from high-end residential projects to museum design, hospitality, commercial and urban renewal projects. Yuri holds a Master Degree in Advanced Architectural Design from Columbia University's Graduate School of Architecture, Planning, and Preservation. She has taught at Parsons The New School for Design.
